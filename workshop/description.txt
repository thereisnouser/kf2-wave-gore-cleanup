[h1]Wave Gore Cleanup[/h1]
A small mutator for Killing Floor 2 that clears accumulated blood and corpses at the beginning of each wave.

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*] Removes dead bodies, blood splats, blood pools and cosmetic giblets at wave start.
[*] Cleanup runs locally on each connected client.
[*] New gore can appear normally during combat.
[*] Does not intentionally remove weapons, ammo or cash pickups.
[*] No cheat console commands required.
[/list]

[h1]Installation[/h1]
Mutator: WaveGoreCleanup.WaveGoreCleanupMutator

Steam subscription downloads the content but does not automatically enable the mutator on a dedicated server.

[h1]Compatibility and status[/h1]
Experimental build for modded Survival servers. Local and single-client dedicated server cleanup have been tested. Multiple network clients, automatic package delivery, dropped item persistence and perk progression are still being validated. Not officially whitelisted; Ranked progression is not guaranteed.
